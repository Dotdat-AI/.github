## Manage SEO and AI Visibility in One Place With Dotdat AI.AI

[Dotdat.AI](https://dotdat.ai/) is an all-in-one AI marketing platform for SEO, content creation, and AI search visibility. It helps businesses publish content, monitor search performance, and track their brand across search engines and AI assistants.

### Features for SEO and AI Marketing

Dotdat.AI brings content creation, SEO, and AI visibility tools into one dashboard. You can research topics, generate blog articles and hero images, and publish content immediately or on a schedule.

It also offers keyword tracking, competitor analysis, backlink monitoring, website audits, and automatic content submission to Google and Bing. For AI search, you can track brand mentions across ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini and compare your visibility with competitors. Multiple languages, custom domains, and team collaboration are also supported.

### How It Works

Connect your website, research topics, and create articles using AI. Review and publish your content or schedule it for later. New posts are automatically submitted to Google and Bing.

You can then use the dashboard to monitor keyword rankings, backlinks, competitors, indexing status, and AI visibility across your websites.

### Who Is It For?

Dotdat.AI is designed for businesses, SEO professionals, digital marketing agencies, and content teams managing one or multiple websites. It's useful for those who want to manage content publishing, SEO, and AI search visibility without relying on several separate tools.
